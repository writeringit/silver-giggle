## 1. Purpose
This SOP explains how to generate and share the weekly sales report
from the Sales Dashboard, so every report is consistent and accurate.

## 2. Scope
Applies to team members who prepare the weekly report. Does not cover
dashboard setup or data-source configuration.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
- **Report preparer:** generates and checks the report
- **Team lead:** reviews and approves before sharing

## 4. Before you begin
- You have a stable internet connection and a supported browser (Chrome or Edge)
- You have access to the Sales Dashboard
- You know the reporting week (Monday to Sunday)
- You know the employees in your team

## 5. Procedure
1. Log in to the Sales Dashboard.
2. Select **Reports** from the left-hand menu.
3. Choose the **Weekly Sales** report template.
4. Set the date range to the previous Monday through Sunday.
5. Click **Generate report**.
6. Check that total orders and total revenue are not zero.
7. Export the report as a PDF.
8. Send the PDF to the team lead for approval.

## 6. Troubleshooting
| Problem | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Report shows no data | Wrong date range | Re-check the dates in step 4 |
| Export button is greyed out | Report not finished loading | Wait 30 seconds and try again |
| Report total looks too high | Same order counted twice | Remove duplicate orders in the filter, then regenerate the report |
